Select one clear object

Tap an object or drag a rectangle around it, leaving a little background. You can also use Select center with a keyboard. Keep the camera still.

Pixels, not a 3D reconstruction

This tool extrapolates short-term movement in the image. It does not infer collisions or material properties. Occlusion or camera motion can interrupt tracking.

Your images stay here

Tracking runs on your device, without Meta model calls. Exported measurements contain positions and scores, not camera images. Meta’s verified intervention proposals are in the Decision lab.
