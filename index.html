What Meta does

Muse Spark 1.3 receives a supplied scene and a bounded command space. It proposes a command, receives measured feedback, then revises its proposal. The public example preserves all three actual provider responses.

What physics does

The local engine computes planar collisions and contact energy. A separate search compares 37 commands. Neither the solver nor the 3D rendering is a Meta model.

What this proves

A model proposal can be tested, rejected and improved in a known simulation. Initial states are supplied; contact shapes are discs and commands change velocity instantly. The 8 J threshold belongs to this experiment.

This replay is not a measurement of real-world prediction accuracy or a demonstration of physical robot control.
