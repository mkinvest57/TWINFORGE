demo conditions: (1) a twin whose physics was INDUCED from observations by the
agent (parameters estimated from observed velocities only, contact samples
rejected, restitution measured at the bounce — no scripted lerp toward true
values), (2) a ghost forecast projected +0.5s AHEAD of the ball at all times,
(3) an "in / out" verdict issued and locked BEFORE the ball lands,
(4) reproducible several times in a row, with real-vs-predicted error displayed.

**Protocol (execute in order, no tuning between shots):**
1. Run Level 1 on a series of fresh shots never used for induction.
2. Per shot, record: locked verdict (timestamped, before landing), real outcome,
   ghost error in cm, ghost ahead (+0.5s) for the whole flight, ball ID
   continuity through the rim (FP-01), frames dropped for blur (FP-02).
3. Compare induced parameters to the true ones ONLY on the evaluation side, afterwards.
4. Series balance: correct-verdict rate, mean error, failures named by failure point (FP-01…FP-06).
5. Per-scene checklist: launch, bounce, hoop passage and net where present,
   ghost ahead (same physics model as the real), induction, control — one failed
   test = scene not validated, whatever the global verdict.
